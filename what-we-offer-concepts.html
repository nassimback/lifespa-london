Homepage concepts: 14 options

A1–A7: "What we offer". B1–B7: "Most booked". Each uses the brief's copy. Every treatment stays one click from the homepage, with one action per section and no repeated buttons. Pick one per section (or mix two) and I'll drop it into the homepage.

1Vertical tabs

Closest to the original tabs. Group names stay visible in a side list, so there is one click to reach any group. Photo and treatment links on the right. Familiar, easy to scan.

What we offer

2Photo tiles

Most visual, least text. Five tall photo cards. Treatment links appear on hover (always shown on mobile). The whole tile goes to the group menu.

What we offer

3Plus/minus accordion

Matches Jo's favourite pattern (the + rows on luminisbeauty.co.uk), and the same pattern the service pages use. Very tidy, closed by default. No photos.

What we offer

4Editorial list + hover preview

Big, calm typography. Large group names; hovering one swaps the photo beside it. Each row links straight to its menu. Fewest elements on screen.

What we offer

5Split viewer (dark)

Strongest contrast, one compact block. Photo on the left, group names on the right. Pick a group to see its two-line intro and links. Adds a black band between white sections.

6Text columns (no photos)

Fastest to use, one click to every treatment. All five groups and every treatment visible at once, like a menu card. Minimal, but the least visual.

What we offer

7Swipe cards

Best on phones. A row of cards you swipe or scroll sideways, each with a photo, two-line intro and links. Keeps the section short on mobile.
